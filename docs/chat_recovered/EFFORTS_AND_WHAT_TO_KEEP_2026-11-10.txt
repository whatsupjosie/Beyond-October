EFFORTS SO FAR, AND WHAT TO KEEP FROM EACH
A running harvest. 2026-11-10, by Claude Fable 5.2 for the owner of Rearview Foresight. Plain text so it can be copied whole.
Add to it as more efforts are shared. Nothing here is a build instruction.

MARKS: READ = I read it (often in part; my other files say which parts).  SEEN = I read only its outline or its own notes.  TOLD = known only from a conversation or handoff about it.  NOT SEEN = I have never had it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING THE PERSON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Affect context engine (affect_context_engine.py)   READ
    Keep: a person is judged against their own normal; doubt belongs to how well she knows them; every reading says what would prove it wrong; a budget of three probes; the check "maybe I caused this"; holding forever is not allowed.
 2. Disambiguation engine (eq_disambiguation_engine.py)   SEEN
    Keep: ask who is experiencing it, what it is about, and in what part of life, before choosing a response. Its own example: "I took a shot" against "I was shot."
 3. System Alex's judge (emotional_orchestrator.py)   READ
    Keep: one judge for all evidence; every signal says where it came from and when it expires; disagreement between sources is kept and lowers confidence; nothing clinical decides alone.
 4. Empathy pass (empathy_layer.py, population_prior.py)   TOLD (its handoff only)
    Keep: a stated "I'm not okay" is heard on the first message; a "fine" that does not fit only opens a door; knowing someone takes real days, not just turns; the list of needs (be heard, steadiness, room to think, practical help, encouragement, celebrate with, light company).
 5. The research mode's care routines (care_routines.py)   READ (engine parts)
    Keep: read shape when words cannot be trusted; presence is the floor; three tries that do not help means hold steady; play needs confident calm; silence is never relief.
 6. PEQ and CRE-EQ   NOT SEEN
 7. Research digest   READ
    Keep: the signals are weak, so the gain is in knowing when she does not know.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHOOSING WHAT TO DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8. Alex core, 5.6 (alex_core.py)   READ (opening)
    Keep: "Mirror, Not Hammer"; five ways of being present (guide, companion, mirror, anchor, witness); a sense of how much the person has left in them.
 9. Response cards (eq_card_selector.py, eq_integration_layer.py)   SEEN
    Keep: match the response to context, who it is about and how strong it is, not to an emotion word. Leave behind: canned questions and hotline text.
10. The Director and its agents   TOLD (the transcript only)
    Keep: one place that decides between presence and challenge; a watch against parroting the person back; decisions that can be inspected.
11. The routine engine inside the care routines   READ
    Keep: a routine is an intent, ordered beats and example lines to adapt, never recite; never repeat while others remain; score the next turn; favour what worked.
12. Friend core (friend_core_package)   READ
    Keep: the order. Memory, insight, pushback, encouragement, what's next. Pushback "Respectfully challenges assumptions without insisting."
13. Acceptance engine (acceptance_engine.py)   READ
    Keep: "Reality first. Then movement." And its lists of what not to say at each point.

==========================================================================
FOUR THREADS THAT RUN THROUGH ALL OF IT
==========================================================================
1. Every effort reached the same truth by its own road: understand the situation before choosing the response. That is strong evidence the direction is right.
2. Each effort made its own words for the same things. There are at least four vocabularies for "who is this about" and "is it literal." One shared set of words would let the pieces talk to each other.
3. Most of the good ideas are not connected to a live conversation. The gap is wiring more than invention.
4. Every reader of meaning so far, including my first one, uses word patterns. That is the one ingredient none of the efforts has yet: a model reading the meaning, with the machinery around it doing what these efforts already do well.
